The rebase failure ('column already exists' after migrations were renumbered) is recorded with the rule to regenerate the migration after a rebase rather than keep its old number, and a reader of the existing 'relation does not exist' entry can find it: either as a dated line directly beneath that entry, or as a separate entry plus a dated See also / Evidence line beneath the existing entry that points to it.
